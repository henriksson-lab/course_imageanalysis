Interactive demos

Every interactive demo in the course, in teaching order. Each one also appears in its unit’s exercise, together with questions to explore.

Transforming an image with a 2×2 matrix

Drag the two columns of the matrix and watch the image follow. Unit: Images as arrays & geometric transforms.

3×3 kernels

Edit nine weights and see the filtered image live; click a pixel to see the weighted sum. Unit: Filtering & convolution.

Build a wave from five frequencies

Unit: Images in frequency space.

Filter an image in frequency space

Keep or remove parts of the 2D spectrum and see the image rebuilt. Unit: Images in frequency space.

The shape of a point cloud: PCA

Drag the points of a cloud of two-pixel images and a direction through it: centring, the covariance ellipse, the principal components, projection and reconstruction error. Unit: Principal component analysis.

Quantisation and dithering

Round an image to 1–8 bits, with or without dither, and see the error and its histogram. Unit: Quantisation & bit depth.

How many bits does a camera need?

A simulated camera (full well, read noise, ADC bits, exposure) and its noise-versus-signal plot. Unit: Camera noise, SNR and PSNR.

Showing a 12-bit image: windows, wrap-around, CT windows

Unit: 12- and 16-bit images in practice.

The bits of a float

Unit: Floating point.

Summing many pixels in float32 and float16

Unit: Floating point.

The energy of a line profile

Segment a noisy 1D profile: thresholding against the labelling with the smallest energy, found exactly by dynamic programming. Unit: Segmentation as energy minimisation.

Maximum flow, one augmenting path at a time

Step through Ford–Fulkerson on a small graph, see the residual graph, and the minimum cut at the end. Unit: Graphs, cuts and maximum flow.

The graph of a small image

Pixels as nodes, t-links and n-links with their capacities, and the minimum cut; make your own cut and compare. Unit: Graph-cut segmentation.

Interactive graph cut

Paint object and background seeds and see the segmentation update. Unit: Graph-cut segmentation.

Fiedler vectors and normalized cuts

Split points or a small image with the second eigenvector of the graph Laplacian. Unit: Spectral partitioning and normalized cuts.

Fit a line scan with a small neural network

Train a 1 → H → 1 ReLU network live and watch the hinges move. Unit: Neural networks.

PCA as a linear autoencoder

Turn a one-number linear autoencoder on two-pixel images; train a nonlinear one on a curve. Unit: From PCA to autoencoders.

The VAE loss in one dimension

Move the encoder’s cloud and see reconstruction, KL and their sum. Unit: The variational autoencoder.

Generating images with a VAE

Encode, decode, walk and sample in the 2D latent space of handwritten digits and of synthetic cell shapes. Unit: Generating and exploring images.

GPU texture formats

The same data in two texture formats side by side, stretched so the steps show; nearest or linear filtering (needs WebGL2). Unit: How graphics cards store colour.
